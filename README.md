To use the program just make sure salaries.csv is in the same folder as main.go, then open a terminal in the KamsiOgbuzeProject2 folder and run go run main.go. If you run it in GoLand make sure the working directory is this folder or it won't find the file. There's no menu, it just prints everything and ends.

The output is split into five parts and each one has a header. Part 1 shows the average entry level salary for 2024 and 2023. Part 2 shows the median for both years, how much it went up or down, and how much higher the average is than the median, which means a few really high salaries are pulling the average up. Part 3 shows how much of a raise you could get going from entry level to mid level in 2024. Part 4 compares remote and on-site medians for 2024, first for entry level and then for all levels. Part 5 shows the highest paying entry level job title in 2024 and the next four, not counting anything with Research in the title. All the salaries are in US dollars.

Red means the comparison went the bad way, like 2024 being lower than 2023 or on-site paying more than remote. Green means it went the other way. Cyan is just info and isn't comparing anything. In Part 5 the top job is green and the next four are yellow.

I finished everything so nothing is left out.
